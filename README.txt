DIGITAL AGENCY WEBSITE
======================

Files:
- index.html : page content and sections
- style.css  : responsive premium dark styling
- script.js  : mobile menu, reveal animations, contact form and contact settings

HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open the digital_agency_website folder.
3. Double-click index.html. It should open in your browser.
4. An internet connection is needed for the Google Fonts.

CUSTOMIZE BEFORE PUBLISHING
1. Open script.js in a text editor.
2. Replace "Your Agency" with your final agency name.
3. Review the email and WhatsApp number in the AGENCY settings.
4. The contact form prepares an email in the visitor's email app; it does not send messages to a server.
5. Replace the sample portfolio concepts with your own work. They are clearly identified as concepts.
6. Add your real social media links and business details if desired.

IMPORTANT PRIVACY NOTE
The supplied email address and WhatsApp number are included because they were requested.
Before making this site public, consider replacing personal contact details with dedicated
business contact details approved by a parent/guardian. Do not publish a home address.

This is a static starter website. It does not include a database, online payments,
server-side form handling, analytics, or a content management system.
